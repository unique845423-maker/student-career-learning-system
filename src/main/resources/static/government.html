🎯 Understanding Eligibility

🎂

Age

Government opportunities may have minimum and maximum age requirements. Always check the official notification.

📚

Educational Qualification

Required qualification can vary from school-level education to diploma, graduation or higher qualifications.

🪪

Documents

Applicants may need identity documents, educational certificates, photographs and other documents specified in the official notification.

📋

Other Requirements

Some opportunities may have additional requirements. Read the complete official notification before applying.

📚 Required Qualification

🏫

School Level

Some government scholarships and student schemes are available for school students.

💻

Diploma

Some opportunities may accept diploma qualifications. Eligibility depends on the specific notification.

🎓

Graduation

Many government examinations and career opportunities specify graduation or an equivalent qualification.

🚀

Higher Qualification

Certain specialised opportunities may require postgraduate or professional qualifications.

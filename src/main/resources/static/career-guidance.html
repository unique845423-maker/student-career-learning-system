🧭 Understand Your Pathway

Different students can follow different routes toward higher education and careers.

🎓 Class 9–10

Focus on strong fundamentals and explore different subjects and career areas.

  • Build basic academic skills
  • Explore interests
  • Understand future streams
  • Develop communication and digital skills

🔬 Class 11–12

Choose an appropriate academic pathway and start exploring higher-study options.

  • Science / Commerce / Arts
  • PCM / PCB / PCMB
  • Entrance preparation where relevant
  • Course and college research

🛠️ Diploma / ITI

Technical and skill-based pathways can provide practical learning and further-study opportunities.

  • Technical skills
  • Practical training
  • Industry-oriented learning
  • Higher-study options

💻 Technology

Technology careers require continuous learning and practical skill development.

  • Programming
  • Data and databases
  • Networks and systems
  • Projects and portfolios

💼 Business

Business pathways combine financial, analytical, communication and management skills.

  • Accounting
  • Finance
  • Economics
  • Management

🎨 Creative Fields

Creative careers can combine design, communication, technology and visual thinking.

  • Graphic design
  • UI/UX
  • Media
  • Architecture and design
